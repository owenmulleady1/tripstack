# TripStack — coursework prototype

A mobile-friendly fictional group-trip planning concept with a destination discovery interface and a functional demo.

## Deploy to GitHub Pages
1. Create a **new public repository** called `tripstack` (do not reuse previous coursework repos).
2. Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, `demo.html`, `demo.css`, and `demo.js` to the repository root.
3. In **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, `main`, `/ (root)` and save.
4. Landing page: `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/tripstack/`; demo: `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/tripstack/demo.html`.
5. Test both URLs in an incognito window and on a phone. Check itinerary additions, votes, expenses, refresh persistence and reset.

## Important limitations
- The reels are **illustrated, reel-inspired destination cards**, not playable videos.
- All eight creator handles, all sample activities and expenses, and all trip participants are fictional.
- Each destination has its own localStorage workspace. No real-time sharing across devices.
- No real creator partnership, holiday supplier link, payment, travel booking, or live collaboration exists.
- The demo form on the landing page does not transmit or store submitted data.
- Actual testing by someone other than the builder and resulting changes **must be documented truthfully by the student** after testing.
- The required 150–300 word reflection must be authored entirely by the student, not generated or rewritten by AI.
- The assignment's submission instructions appear contradictory (Moodle text only vs Word/PDF with screenshots); check with lecturer/Moodle.
